ClayPT examples

  • Basic: procedural geometry, OpenPBR materials, an area light and an HDR environment.
  • Three.js: a Three.js scene with a glTF model, converted with fromTHREEScene.
  • Animation: a procedural skinned cylinder with bone animation and a morph target.
  • Advanced: OpenPBR layers, shadow catcher, OIDN denoising and offline PNG export.

ClayPT uses WebGPU when available and falls back to WebGL 2.
